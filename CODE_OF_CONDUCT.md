# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Disagree about the work, not about
each other.

## What that means here

Things that make this project better:

- Taking someone's idea seriously before arguing with it.
- Saying "I was wrong" without ceremony.
- Reviewing code, not the person who wrote it.
- Remembering that English is not everyone's first language, and neither is French.

Things that are not welcome, and will be removed:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained derailing, bad-faith argument, or making the project unpleasant on purpose.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to <ocb.ketu@gmail.com>. Reports are handled discreetly and the
reporter's identity is not shared.

Depending on what happened, the response ranges from a private word, to editing or removing
the content, to a temporary or permanent ban from the project. The maintainer decides, and
will explain the reasoning to the people involved.
